  Are you a woman in your late 30s or 40s wondering why your body suddenly feels different? You are not alone.

  In this episode of the Kumneger Podcast, Meron sits down for an open conversation about perimenopause and menopause. Together they explore the physical, emotional, and mental changes many women experience, often without realising what is happening.

  Meron shares a range of natural and complementary approaches that some women find helpful in managing symptoms, from nutrition and lifestyle changes to stress management, sleep, movement, and hormone balance. The conversation offers practical insights to help women navigate this important stage of life.

  This episode is designed to raise awareness and encourage informed conversations within the Ethiopian and Eritrean community. The information shared reflects Meron's own experience and perspective, and is not a substitute for personalised medical advice. If you have concerns about your health or symptoms, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
