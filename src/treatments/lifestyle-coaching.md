  Treatments help enormously, but what happens in the days between them matters just as much. This is where lifestyle coaching comes in.

  ETM is not about quick fixes or perfection. It is about returning to the simple rhythms that help us care for ourselves — and building those rhythms takes support, honesty about what is realistic, and adjustment along the way.

  We work across four areas: rest, movement, nourishment, and mindful living. Rather than handing you a demanding regime, we look at your actual life — the hours you keep, the pressures you carry, what you have already tried — and build something small enough that it holds.
