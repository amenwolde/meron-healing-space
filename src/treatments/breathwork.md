body: |
  Breath is one of the few things in the body we can consciously influence, and one of the quickest routes into genuine calm. It is also, for most people, entirely unexamined.

  These sessions are simple by design. There is nothing strenuous or dramatic here — no forcing, no overwhelming intensity. We work with steady, guided breathing practices that encourage the nervous system to settle and the mind to quieten.

  Alongside the practice itself, we work on awareness: noticing how you breathe when you are stressed, where you hold it, what changes when you let it lengthen. That awareness is what makes the practice useful outside the session.

  Breathwork sessions can be taken on their own or alongside bodywork treatments, and they work well online as well as in person. You leave with a short practice you can genuinely sustain — a few minutes rather than a demanding routine.
